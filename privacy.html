Privacy Policy

FoldSize: Shrink & Fit Files, by STA Design LLC. Effective November 20, 2026.

Short version: FoldSize does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and your files never leave your iPhone or iPad. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and the app does not send anything to our servers. We don't have any.

Your files

Photos, videos, PDFs, documents and folders you add are shrunk, converted, combined, zipped, cleaned up and renamed on your device. Cleaning up scans and making them searchable use Apple's on-device image and text recognition (the same as Live Text); nothing is sent anywhere. While a job runs, FoldSize works on copies in its own temporary folder; the copies are deleted the next time you open the app or the share sheet. The new files go only where you save or share them. FoldSize only sees the photos, videos and files you pick; it doesn't have access to the rest of your library, and it doesn't ask for it. Videos are shrunk with Apple's video encoder on your device.

Safari extension

If you turn on FoldSize in Safari, it looks at pages for file upload buttons and reads the words next to them (such as "Max 5 MB") on your device to find the limit. When you choose a file with Fit with FoldSize, the file is passed from Safari to FoldSize on the same device, fitted, and put back into that page's upload. Nothing is sent to us or anywhere else, and FoldSize doesn't read or keep anything else from the pages you visit. To have the limit ready next time, Safari keeps the site's name and its limit on your device. You can see and forget them in the FoldSize menu in Safari, and deleting the app deletes them.

Settings

FoldSize saves a few settings on your device so it remembers them: your last size limit, whether it's for each file or all files, the file type to save as, whether to remove locations from photos and videos, limits you save by name (such as County permit portal), your rename choices, whether Clean up scans makes PDFs searchable, the last few limits you used in the share sheet and whether it fits right away, and that you've seen the welcome sheet. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save to Files, iOS sends the file only to the app, place or person you choose. FoldSize doesn't see or keep a copy.

Purchases

FoldSize Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether this Apple ID has Pro.

Children

FoldSize does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example a feature that syncs files between devices, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
