Privacy Policy

FoldSize: Shrink & Fit Files, by STA Design LLC. Effective November 14, 2026.

Short version: FoldSize does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and your files never leave your iPhone or iPad. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and the app does not send anything to our servers. We don't have any.

Your files

Photos, PDFs, documents and folders you add are shrunk, converted, combined, zipped and renamed on your device. While a job runs, FoldSize works on copies in its own temporary folder; the copies are deleted the next time you open the app or the share sheet. The new files go only where you save or share them. FoldSize only sees the photos and files you pick; it doesn't have access to the rest of your library.

Error messages and screenshots

When you paste an error message or pick a screenshot of one, the words are read on your device with Apple's built-in text recognition to find the size limit. The text and the screenshot are not saved or sent anywhere.

Settings

FoldSize saves a few settings on your device so it remembers them: your last size limit, whether it's for each file or all files, the file type to save as, whether to remove photo locations, your rename pattern, and that you've seen the welcome cards. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save to Files, iOS sends the file only to the app, place or person you choose. FoldSize doesn't see or keep a copy.

Purchases

FoldSize Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether this Apple ID has Pro.

Children

FoldSize does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example a feature that syncs files between devices, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
