Fitting a file

  • Know the limit? Pick it at the top of the screen (such as 10 MB), choose whether it's for each file or all files together, add your files and tap Fit.
  • An upload just failed? Under Upload failed? Find the limit, read a screenshot of the error or paste its message. FoldSize finds the size limit and the file types the site takes.
  • Check before you upload. Each result has a ready check and a preview. Look through converted pages before sending them.
  • Signed PDFs: changing a digitally signed PDF breaks its signature. Fit the file first, then sign it.

File types

PDF, JPG, HEIC, PNG, GIF, TIFF, WebP and BMP, MOV, MP4 and M4V videos, zips and whole folders. Videos are saved as MP4 (H.264), the kind every site plays. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files are turned into PDFs on your device when they're over the limit, when you choose PDF, or when the site only takes PDF. Very wide spreadsheets may split across pages, and animated GIFs become a still picture.

FoldSize Pro

Free fits one photo or PDF at a time. Pro adds videos, batches, folders, zips (opened, or saved as a zip split into parts that each fit), renaming, Office and text files, combining files into one PDF in your order, and PNG. Pro is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription, a one-time lifetime purchase, or a Day pass that lasts 24 hours and doesn't renew. There's no account.

  • New phone or reinstall? Open FoldSize, tap PRO at the top right, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. It stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
