Fitting a file

  • Set the limit. Type it or tap a size (such as 10 MB), choose whether it's for each file or all files together, add your files and tap Fit.
  • Sending by email? Tap Limits for Gmail, Outlook and more and pick the service. FoldSize sets its limit for you.
  • Same site every time? Set its limit, open the same menu and tap Save this limit. Your limits show at the top next time. They're kept on your device.
  • In Safari: turn on FoldSize in Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions, then tap Fit with FoldSize under an upload button on a web page.
  • From Share: tap Share on a photo or PDF in Photos, Files or Mail, then FoldSize.
  • Check before you upload. Each result has a ready check and a preview. Look through converted pages before sending them.
  • Signed PDFs: changing a digitally signed PDF breaks its signature. Fit the file first, then sign it.

File types

PDF, JPG, HEIC, PNG, GIF, TIFF, WebP and BMP, MOV, MP4 and M4V videos, zips and whole folders. Videos are saved as MP4 (H.264), the kind every site plays. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files are turned into PDFs on your device when they're over the limit, when you choose PDF, or when the site only takes PDF. Very wide spreadsheets may split across pages, and animated GIFs become a still picture.

FoldSize Pro

Free fits one photo or PDF at a time. Pro adds videos, batches, folders, zips (opened, or saved as a zip split into parts that each fit), renaming, Office and text files, combining files into one PDF in your order, cleaning up scans (crop, straighten and whiten photos of pages and scanned PDFs; free shows the before and after), searchable PDFs (the text on scanned pages is found on your device and added, so it can be searched and copied), picking pages out of a PDF, splitting a PDF into parts that each fit, and PNG. Pro is sold through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription, a one-time lifetime purchase, or a Day pass that lasts 24 hours and doesn't renew. There's no account.

  • New phone or reinstall? Open FoldSize, go to the Tools tab, tap FoldSize Pro, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. It stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
