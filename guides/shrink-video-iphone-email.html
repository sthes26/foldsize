How to shrink a video on iPhone to send by email or text

Short answer: a minute of iPhone 4K video can be several hundred MB, while email takes about 18 MB. Lower the resolution to 720p or 1080p and the bitrate to match the limit, and the full video, with sound, fits.

Ways to make it smaller

  1. Record smaller next time. In Settings > Camera > Record Video, choose 1080p at 30 fps instead of 4K.
  2. Trim it. In Photos, tap Edit and drag the ends of the timeline. Every second you cut saves space.
  3. Shrink it to the limit. In FoldSize Pro, add the video from Photos or Files, type the limit or tap Gmail, then tap Fit. It keeps the full length, the sound and the date it was filmed, and saves an MP4 that plays everywhere.
  4. Send a link. Share from iCloud Photos or a cloud drive if the person just needs to watch it.

Before you send

  • Play the shrunk copy once to check faces and text are still clear.
  • Videos with fast motion need more space than talking or slow pans. If it looks blocky, trim it instead of shrinking further.
